## About Spadex :
**SpaDeX** or **Space Docking Experiment** is a twin satellite mission developed by the [Indian Space Research Organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISRO "ISRO") (ISRO) to mature technologies related to orbital [rendezvous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_rendezvous "Space rendezvous"), [docking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spacecraft_Docking_and_Berthing_Mechanisms "Spacecraft Docking and Berthing Mechanisms"), [formation flying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formation_flying "Formation flying"), with scope of applications in human spaceflight, in-space satellite servicing and other proximity operations

The two satellites —
SDX01 (Chaser) and SDX02(Target) — launched by the PSLV C60
