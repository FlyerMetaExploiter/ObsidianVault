
**I. Introduction**

**II. What is Physical/Physiographic Division?**

- It's the division of a region based on a comprehensive assessment of its geographical characteristics.
    
- **Basis for Division:**
    
    - **Rock Structure (चट्टान)**
        
    - **Climate (जलवायु)**
        
    - **Human Habitation (मानव बसावट)**
        
    - **Vegetation & Agriculture (वनस्पति और कृषि)**
        
    - **Soil (मृदा)** (Implied, often linked to rock & vegetation)
        
- It provides a foundational understanding as all other geographical topics (rivers, agriculture, minerals, etc.) are linked to these physical regions.
    

**III. Overview of Chhattisgarh's Physical Divisions**

- Chhattisgarh can be broadly divided into four main physical regions.
    
- **Context:** These divisions relate to larger Indian physiographic features:
    
    - Satpura Range (North/North-West border influence)
        
    - Vindhyachal Range (North border influence)
        
    - Chota Nagpur Plateau (North-East border influence - Ranchi & Palamu parts)
        
    - Dandakaranya Plateau (Southern part of a larger plateau)
        
    - Baghelkhand Plateau (Eastern part of the larger plateau extending into MP/UP)
        
    - Mahanadi Basin originated from ancient depositional basins (Chhattisgarh Basin & Bijawar Basin).

    - **Surguja District Tehsils:** Extends into tehsils like Sitapur, Lundra, Batouli, Mainpat, etc. (**Excludes** Ambikapur, Lakhanpur, Udaipur tehsils).
        
- **C. Mahanadi Basin / CG Plains:**
    
    - Entire Raipur, Bilaspur, Durg Divisions.
        
    - Touches parts of Surguja (Chhuri-Udaipur hills border).

- **D. Dandakaranya Plateau:**
    
    - Entire Bastar Division (Kanker, Kondagaon, Narayanpur, Bastar, Dantewada, Sukma, Bijapur).
        
    - **Note:** While the Godavari basin influence extends to Mohla-Manpur, that specific area is geographically considered part of the Mahanadi Basin's Marginal Highlands, not Dandakaranya proper.

**VI. Detailed Study: Jashpur-Samri Part Pradesh**

- **General:** Smallest division, extension of Chota Nagpur Plateau. River Basins: Brahmani & Kanhar.
    
- **Formation:** Formed in 4 main stages (+ ongoing 5th stage):
    
    1. **Rock Bed Formation:** Initial landmass creation (Deccan Trap rocks).
        
    2. **River Erosion & Dissection:** Rivers cut through the plateau, isolating sections.
        
    3. **Upliftment:** The entire region was uplifted.
        
    4. **Further Erosion:** Rivers deepened valleys, creating characteristic steep scarps ('Part' topography - one steep side, one gentler slope).
        
    5. **Ongoing Erosion:** The process continues today.
        
- **Tribes:** Predominantly Munda group sub-tribes (Birhor, Nagesia, Oraon).
    
- **Specific Parts:**
    
    - **Jashpur Part:** Largest Part.
        
    - **Pandrapat:** Smallest Part. Contains Khurja Hills (Origin of Ib River) & Bakhana Hills (Origin of Kanhar River). Important Places: Sanna ("Switzerland of CG"), Bagicha.
        
    - **Lahsun Part:** Highest Part. Contains **Gaurlata Peak (1225 m)** - Highest peak of Chhattisgarh. Location: Shankargarh & Rajpur tehsils (Balrampur).
        
    - **Jamir Part:** Known as "Bauxite Ground". Location: Samri & Kusmi tehsils (Balrampur).
        
    - **Samri Part:** Often includes Lahsun & Jamir; separated by Kanhar River.
        
    - **Jarang Part:** Located between Jashpur and Mainpat.
        
    - **Mainpat:**
        
        - Height: Approx. 1152 m.
            
        - Known as "Shimla of Chhattisgarh".
            
        - River Boundaries: Ghunghutta (N), Rehar/Mand (E/W influence), Udaipur hills border (S/W).
            
        - **Important Sites:** Maltipur Adventure Zone, Parpatiya (Sunrise Point), Valleys (Paiga, Sarbhanja, Kadam), Caves (Bhalu Maada, Raka Maada), Waterfalls (Sarbhanja, Tiger Point, Fish Point), **Daljali** (Shaking Ground - likely formed over a dried, vegetation-filled lake bed), **Thinthini Patthar** (Resonating Rock - near Mainpat, Darima area).
            
        - **Famous For:** Potatoes (small, red variety), Horticulture (Litchi, Apple trials), **Coffee (Brand: Sogda)**, **Kudareli Bauxite Mine** (Largest in CG).
            
- **Overall Characteristics:**
    
    - **Rock:** Primarily Deccan Trap.
        
    - **Minerals:** **Bauxite** (Abundant), Beryl (Jashpur only).
        
    - **Soil:** Laterite Soil.
        
    - **Agriculture:** Horticulture focus (Coffee, Tea, Litchi, Apple, Jackfruit, Potato). Less traditional grain farming (though some GI-tagged rice like Jeeraphool exists).
        

**VII. Detailed Study: Eastern Baghelkhand Plateau**

- **General:** Located in the North-West of CG. It's the eastern fringe of the larger Baghelkhand Plateau. Separated from Bundelkhand by the Son River.
    
- **Sub-Divisions & Features:**
    
    - **Camur Range:** Extension of Vindhyachal Range. Districts: MCB, Koriya. (Homework: Check extension in Surajpur).
        
    - **Sonhat Plateau:** Districts: Koriya, MCB. (Sonhat tehsil is in Koriya).
        
    - **Chang Bhakar-Devgarh Hills:** Runs West-East like a wall in northern CG. Rock: Red Sandstone. Districts: MCB, Koriya, Surajpur, Balrampur, Surguja. Highest Peak: **Devgarh (1033 m)** in MCB. Divides Surguja Basin into North & South.
        
    - **Koriya/Kurasia & Chirmiri Hills:** Rich in Coal. Part of Hasdeo-Arand Coalfield.
        
    - **Sita Lekhni Hills:** District: Surajpur. Mythological significance (Lakshman teaching Sita letters). (Homework: Locate Kudargarh temple mountain - Chang Bhakar or Sita Lekhni?).
        
    - **Ramgarh Hills:** Alleged meeting point of Vindhyachal & Satpura. Historical & Cultural Importance (Ancient theatre, inscriptions). Sites: Jogimara Cave, Sita Bengra Cave, Lakshman Bengra Cave, Hathi Pol Tunnel. North: Kiskindha Mountain. Location: Surguja district.
        
    - **Hasdeo-Rampur Basin:** Formed by Hasdeo River. Districts: Koriya, MCB, Korba (Poudi Uproda tehsil).
        
    - **Rihand Basin:** Formed by Rihand River. Districts: Balrampur, touches Surajpur border. Extends to Mirzapur (UP). Main Area: Wadrafnagar (Balrampur). Rocks: Kadappa mentioned.
        
    - **Kanhar Basin:** Northernmost part of CG. Formed by Kanhar River. Districts: Balrampur. Extends to UP. Main Area: Ramanujganj, Ramchandrapur tehsils. Rocks: Kadappa mentioned.
        
    - **Surguja Basin:** Broad basin area, divided by Chang Bhakar.
        
- **Overall Characteristics:**
    
    - **Rock:** Primarily **Gondwana** (hence coal), also Dharwar & Kadappa in places.
        
    - **Minerals:** **Coal** is the major mineral.
        
    - **Soil:** Generally less fertile due to Gondwana base, Red-Yellow soil supports some agriculture.
        
    - **Agriculture:** Less intensive. Known for Oilseeds (Sunflower - Surajpur, Balrampur), Maize (Balrampur).
        
    - **Tribes:** Kol, Pando.
        
    - **Forests/Wildlife:** Deciduous forests. Important Fauna: Elephants, Tigers, **Neelgai (Blue Bull)**, Wild Asses (mentioned for Balrampur), Wild Horses (mentioned for Jashpur).
        
    - **Protected Areas:** Guru Ghasidas National Park (shares area with MP), Tamor Pingla WLS, Semarsot WLS.
        
    - **Tourist Spots:** Mentioned under specific hills (Ramgarh, Sita ki Rasoi in MCB), Amritdhara waterfall, झुमका आइलैंड (Jhumka Island - Koriya, Hasdeo River).
