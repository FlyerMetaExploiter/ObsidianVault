## **Sugar Industry in Chhattisgarh: Production, Distribution, and Possibilities**

**Overview:**

Chhattisgarh has a developing sugar industry primarily based on the cooperative model, with Public-Private Partnerships (PPP) also playing a role. Currently, there are four operational sugar mills, with one more proposed. These mills are significant not only for sugar production but also for co-generation of power and ethanol production from by-products.

**Operational Sugar Mills:**

1. **Bhoramdeo Cooperative Sugar Factory Limited (भोरमदेव सहकारी शक्कर कारखाना मर्यादित)**
    
    - **Location:** Ramhepur, Kawardha (Kabirdham District)
        
    - **Established:** 2002-03
        
    - **Significance:**
        
        - Chhattisgarh's first sugar mill.
            
        - Considered the largest operational mill (as per the transcript's emphasis).
            
    - **Power Co-generation:** 6 MW (Megawatts) generated from by-products (bagasse).
        
    - **Ethanol Production:** 80 KLPD (Kilo Litres Per Day).
        
    - **Operating Model:** Public-Private Partnership (PPP) between the Chhattisgarh State Government and Chhattisgarh Slurry Biofuels Private Limited.
        
2. **Maa Mahamaya Sugar Factory Limited (मां महामाया शक्कर कारखाना मर्यादित)**
    
    - **Location:** Kerta Gram, Surajpur District (Transcript notes Google might show Ambikapur, but confirms Kerta Gram).
        
    - **Established:** May 2007
        
    - **Significance:** Chhattisgarh's second sugar mill.
        
    - **Power Co-generation:** 6 MW generation is proposed from by-products.
        
    - **Ethanol Production:** 40 KLPD.
        
    - **Operating Model:** Public-Private Partnership (PPP).
        
3. **Danteshwari Maiyya Cooperative Sugar Factory Limited (दंतेश्वरी मैया सहकारी शक्कर कारखाना मर्यादित)**
    
    - **Location:** Karka Bhatha, Balod District
        
    - **Established:** 2010
        
    - **Significance:** Chhattisgarh's third sugar mill.
        
    - **Power Co-generation:** 3 MW generation is proposed from by-products.
        
    - **Ethanol Production:** Not explicitly mentioned in the transcript.
        
    - **Operating Model:** Cooperative (implied by name).
        
4. **Sardar Vallabh Bhai Patel Cooperative Sugar Factory Limited (सरदार वल्लभ भाई पटेल सहकारी शक्कर कारखाना मर्यादित)**
    
    - **Location:** Visheshra Gram, Pandariya, Kabirdham District (Transcript clarifies location).
        
    - **Established:** 2016
        
    - **Significance:** Chhattisgarh's fourth sugar mill.
        
    - **Power Co-generation:** 14 MW generation proposed or potentially operational from by-products (Highest proposed capacity).
        
    - **Ethanol Production:** Not explicitly mentioned in the transcript.

**Key Takeaways from Transcript:**

- **Current Number:** 4 operational sugar mills.
    
- **Distribution:** Mills are located in Kabirdham (2), Surajpur (1), and Balod (1) districts. A potential new mill is planned for Bemetara.
    
- **Co-generation:** All mills utilize or plan to utilize by-products (primarily bagasse) for power generation (ranging from 3 MW to 14 MW proposed).
    
- **Ethanol Production:** At least two mills (Bhoramdeo and Maa Mahamaya) are actively producing ethanol, contributing to the biofuel sector.
    
- **Operating Models:** A mix of Cooperative and PPP models exists.
    

**Possibilities and Further Insights:**

1. **Ethanol Blending Programme (EBP):** The ethanol production (80 KLPD + 40 KLPD = 120 KLPD from just two mills) directly supports India's EBP goals, aiming to reduce crude oil imports and carbon emissions. There's potential to increase this capacity at existing mills or through new ones.
    
2. **Energy Security:** The co-generation of power makes the sugar mills partially energy self-sufficient and allows them to feed surplus power into the state grid, contributing to rural electrification and grid stability using a renewable biomass source (bagasse). The 14 MW proposed at the Sardar Patel mill is particularly significant.
    
3. **Value Addition & By-product Utilization:**
    
    - **Bagasse:** Used for power generation (co-generation). Could also be explored for paper/pulp or particle board manufacturing.
        
    - **Molasses:** The primary feedstock for ethanol production. Also used in distilleries for potable alcohol or sold as cattle feed supplement.
        
    - **Press Mud:** Can be composted and used as bio-fertilizer in sugarcane fields, improving soil health and reducing chemical fertilizer dependency.
        
4. **Rural Economy & Farmer Income:** Sugar mills are crucial economic hubs in agrarian areas. They provide an assured market for sugarcane farmers, impacting livelihoods and potentially driving rural development. Timely payment and fair pricing (Fair and Remunerative Price - FRP) are critical factors.
    
5. **Expansion Potential:** The proposed mill in Bemetara indicates potential for expanding the sugar industry's footprint in Chhattisgarh, provided sugarcane cultivation is sufficient and supportive policies continue. Kabirdham district, with two mills, appears to be a major sugarcane belt.
    
6. **Water Management:** Sugarcane is a water-intensive crop. Sustainable water management practices are essential for the long-term viability of the industry, especially considering Chhattisgarh's climate.
    
7. **Modernization and Efficiency:** Continuous investment in modernizing mill technology can improve sugar recovery rates, energy efficiency in co-generation, and the efficiency of ethanol production.
    
8. **Policy Influence:** Government policies regarding sugarcane pricing, ethanol procurement, power purchase agreements (for co-generated power), and support for cooperative movements heavily influence the industry's profitability and growth. The transcript's note about the potential impact of government change highlights this dependency.
