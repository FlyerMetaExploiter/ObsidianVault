
Chhattisgarh, known as the _"Tribal Heart of India,"_ is home to over **42 scheduled tribes**, constituting about **30%** of the state's population. The tribal communities have a rich cultural heritage, unique traditions, and a deep connection with nature.
## **Major Tribes of Chhattisgarh**

1. **Gond** – Largest tribal group, known for their **Gond art**, music, and dance.
    
2. **Baiga** – Primitive tribe with rich knowledge of herbal medicine and shifting cultivation (_Bewar_).
    
3. **Bhil** – Known for **archery skills** and traditional dance forms like _Ghoomar_.
    
4. **Halba** – Skilled in **agriculture and handicrafts**.
    
5. **Dhurwa** – Known for their **wooden crafts** and traditional dance (_Karma_).
    
6. **Maria & Muria** – Famous for their **Ghotul system** (youth dormitory) and vibrant festivals.
    
7. **Oraon (Kurukh)** – Known for **Karma Naach** and traditional farming practices.
## **Cultural Aspects of Chhattisgarh Tribes**

### **1. Language & Literature**

- Most tribes speak **local dialects** like **Gondi, Halbi, Chhattisgarhi, and Kurukh**.
    
- **Oral traditions** include folk tales, songs (_Dadaria, Sua_), and proverbs.
    

### **2. Festivals & Dances**

- **Karma** – Celebrated for good harvest (Baiga, Oraon tribes).
    
- **Hareli** – A monsoon festival honoring livestock and agriculture.
    
- **Bastar Dussehra** – A **75-day-long** unique tribal festival (longest in the world).
    
- **Dances:**
    
    - **Sua Nacha** (Parrot Dance) – Performed by women.
        
    - **Panthi** – Spiritual dance of the Satnami community.
        
    - **Raut Nacha** – Performed by Yadav community during Diwali.
        

### **3. Art & Craft**

- **Gond Paintings** – Vibrant depictions of nature and mythology.
    
- **Bastar Art** – Dhokra (metal casting), terracotta, and bell metal crafts.
    
- **Bamboo & Wood Crafts** – Used in daily life and decorations.
    

### **4. Traditional Clothing & Jewelry**

- **Men:** _Lungi_ (dhoti) with _Kurta_ or _Mirjai_ (jacket).
    
- **Women:** _Lugda_ (sari) with _Polkha_ (blouse).
    
- **Jewelry:** Made of beads, silver, and cowrie shells (_Kardhan, Choora_).
    

### **5. Food & Cuisine**

- **Staple Food:** Rice, _Kosra_ (millet), and maize.
    
- **Tribal Dishes:**
    
    - **Chousela** – Rice flour pancakes.
        
    - **Bafauri** – Steamed lentil dumplings.
        
    - **Red Ant Chutney** (_Chaprah_) – A spicy delicacy in Bastar.
        
    - **Mahua Liquor** – Traditional alcoholic drink from Mahua flowers.
        

### **6. Religious Beliefs & Practices**

- **Animism & Nature Worship** – Worship of trees, rivers, and stones.
    
- **Deities:**
    
    - **Danteshwari Devi** (Bastar’s Kuldevi).
        
    - **Budi Mai** (Earth Goddess).
        
    - **Thakur Dev** (Village deity).
        
- **Rituals:** _Mati Puja_ (soil worship), _Narayan Puja_ (for rain).

### **1. Social Structure & Governance**

- **Autonomous Rule:** Many tribes follow **traditional panchayats** (e.g., **Gond Panchayat**).
    
- **Ghotul System (Muria Gond):** A **youth-centric institution** where boys and girls learn life skills, music, and ethical values.
    

### **2. Religious Beliefs & Rituals**

- **Animism & Nature Worship:**
    
    - **Sacred Groves (Devgudi):** Forests dedicated to deities.
        
    - **Stone & River Worship:** **Ganga Maiya (river goddess)** revered.
        
- **Major Deities:**
    
    - **Danteshwari Devi** (Guardian of Bastar).
        
    - **Budi Mai** (Earth Goddess).
        
    - **Thakur Dev** (Village protector).
        
- **Rituals:**
    
    - **Mati Puja** (Soil worship before farming).
        
    - **Narayan Puja** (For rain and prosperity).

### **. Impact of Colonialism & Modern History**

- **British rule** disrupted traditional tribal governance, leading to land alienation.
    
- **Forest Laws (1865, 1878, 1927)** restricted tribal access to forests, sparking rebellions.
    
- **Tana Bhagat Movement (1914-19)** – An Oraon tribal uprising against British oppression.

## **2. Tribal Rebellions & Resistance Movements**

### **Bhoomkal Rebellion (1910)**

- **Cause:** Against British land revenue policies and exploitation.
    
- **Leaders:** **Gundadhur (Gond Leader)** & other tribal chiefs.
    
- **Outcome:** Violent suppression but inspired future movements.
    

### **Other Notable Uprisings**

- **Halba Rebellion (1774-79):** Against Maratha oppression.
    
- **Tarabhumi Movement (1920s):** Tribal resistance for land rights.
    
- **Naxalite Movement (Post-1967):** Some tribal areas became active in left-wing extremism due to neglect.
